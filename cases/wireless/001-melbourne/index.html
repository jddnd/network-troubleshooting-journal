1. Scope

This document records troubleshooting of intermittent Wi-Fi interruptions reported during Microsoft Teams calls at the Melbourne office.

The investigation scope includes client roaming, 802.11r Fast Transition, RF cell overlap, RRM/TPC behavior, 802.1X/EAP-TLS authentication, AP/controller control-path health and endpoint wireless behavior.

4. Reported Symptoms

  • Intermittent Microsoft Teams audio/video interruption.
  • User perception of Wi-Fi disconnects without a consistent visible warning.
  • One Windows WLAN report captured an 802.1X failure stating that the authenticator was no longer present.
Interpretation boundary The Windows message confirms loss of the 802.1X authenticator from the supplicant perspective. It does not by itself prove that the AP physically failed or stopped responding.

10. Current Technical Assessment

Probable root cause Excessive client roaming associated with strong 5 GHz RF cell overlap is the leading explanation for the observed instability. User impact is amplified on clients that negotiate non-FT 802.1X and therefore perform a full EAP-TLS exchange during each roam.

This assessment is not yet confirmed. Confirmation requires after-change evidence from normal office usage showing reduced roam frequency and improved user experience without unacceptable loss of RF coverage.

11. Outstanding Actions

  1. Observe a normal office period with the 14 dBm 5 GHz maximum.
  2. Compare post-change roam count and AP/BSSID transition frequency with pre-change traces.
  3. Check the original affected endpoint when it is present in the office and identify its negotiated AKM.
  4. Determine why the known non-FT client selected AKM 5 while comparable clients select FT AKM 3.
  5. If excessive roaming continues, review Melbourne-specific steering features individually before making additional changes.
  6. Keep historical CAPWAP instability as a separate hypothesis unless a new incident correlates by timestamp.
