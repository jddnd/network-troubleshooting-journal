How entries are classified

Discipline · Aspect · Platform

Discipline identifies the networking domain. Aspect identifies what is being examined. Platform identifies where it is implemented.

Vendor products do not define the discipline. For example, SD-WAN path selection belongs under Routing whether the platform is Cisco SD-WAN or Fortinet Secure SD-WAN.
